Design Horizon: routes into systems practice

Design Horizon is Ruth Guerra’s free resource library, developed through Phenomena Co-Design. It connects systemic, transition, regenerative, values-based and other design approaches. Its About page says the copy and resource selection are human-curated and suggestions are reviewed by hand.

Benjamin P Taylor highlighted it in a SysCoI post on 11 October 2026. The eight selections below offer useful routes into systems practice. The short descriptions summarise the inspected source pages; the questions and atlas cross-links are editorial invitations, not claims of influence or evidence that a method works.
