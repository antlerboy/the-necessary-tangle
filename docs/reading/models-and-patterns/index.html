What can a model show?

A pattern is a question for explanation. These four papers build different model mechanisms: spatial differences that grow, trajectories that separate, local moves that alter a distribution, and reciprocal strategies that can persist. Their sequence here is editorial. It establishes no influence between the authors and no single organisational law.

The four existing bibliography entries now have source-located accounts. Model statements and reported examples are distinguished from fictional practice questions. New exposition remains research for the curator's editorial review. A public reading copy does not grant permission to redistribute its PDF.

What amplifies a spatial difference?

The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis studies coupled reactions and diffusion. Ask which departures from a homogeneous state can grow, under which reaction rates, exchange conditions, and geometry. The early instability calculation does not itself specify the final nonlinear form.

The twenty-cell numerical example uses hypothetical chemistry and a changing catalyst parameter. It shows why the path through parameter values matters: a final patterned equilibrium need not imply that the final homogeneous equilibrium is linearly unstable. Read the located example before using 'self-organisation' as its explanation.

Fictional editorial inquiry. Suppose initially similar service teams develop different workload patterns. Name a local amplification rule and a cross-team exchange rule, with units and a network. What observation would reject the proposed mechanism? A resemblance to a concentration pattern is not evidence that the teams obey diffusion equations.

How long is a detailed projection useful?

Deterministic Nonperiodic Flow separates deterministic rules from practical predictability. Its three equations are a deliberately severe truncation of convection. The bounded, irregular numerical example does not establish a forecasting horizon for the atmosphere or for a service.

At σ=10 and b=8/3, the stated steady-convection instability threshold is r=470/19, about 24.74; the example uses r=28. The article also qualifies the mathematical status of the inference from its finite numerical segment. Parameter values and the evidential qualification belong with the celebrated result.

Fictional editorial inquiry. In an explicitly specified demand, capacity, and backlog model, perturb plausible measurements and parameters. How far ahead do trajectories stay within a decision-relevant tolerance? Could a useful aggregate remain predictable after a detailed trajectory does not? First test the service model; the convection example supplies no organisational equations.

Which local rules produce this distribution?

Dynamic Models of Segregation contains several model structures, not one universal checkerboard rule. A move changes the neighbours and choices of others. Acceptance thresholds, available vacancies, reference neighbourhoods, population proportions, and movement order are separate inputs.

The famous illustrated grid outcome is a particular artificial run. It does not measure a community or establish that discriminatory preferences, institutions, or coercion are irrelevant. Similar-looking patterns can have different causes, and satisfaction with a model rule is not a judgement of justice.

Fictional editorial inquiry. Work with abstract tokens. Declare the boundaries, vacancies, acceptance threshold, tie-breaking order, and stopping rule. Vary the initial arrangement and threshold. Record a cycle or lack of a suitable vacancy rather than build convergence into the conclusion. Any account of an actual organisation needs evidence about its rules, categories, power, and alternatives.

What sustains a reciprocal response?

The Evolution of Cooperation distinguishes success in tournaments, stability against invasion, and initial viability. Tit for Tat starts cooperatively and then copies the partner's preceding move. Its result depends on the game, opponents, repeated encounters, recognition, and interaction pattern.

With the paper's illustrative payoffs T=5,R=3,P=1,S=0, its two stability thresholds for the continuation probability are 1/2 and 2/3. The editorial arithmetic means that 0.8 meets both and 0.5 does not. It does not mean that cooperation must arise: persistent defection can also remain stable in the specified setting.

Fictional editorial inquiry. Give two abstract players the stipulated points and a 0.8 chance of another round. Compare the reciprocal strategy with itself and unconditional defection, then declare observation error as a new variant. What changed in the assumptions? Do not use an abstract tournament as evidence for retaliating against colleagues or service users.

What would make this model applicable?

For each explanation, record the entities, boundary, state variables, mechanism, parameter regime, and measurement. State what the model leaves out and a result that would count against it. An invented exercise tests an inference; it is not evidence that an intervention worked.
