What the connections claim

Primary-source exposition records what a specified passage says. Editorial comparisons bring passages into conversation without claiming that one author influenced another. Both retain visible research status; publication does not invent a named curator’s acceptance.

In particular, an entropy-based regulation theorem does not establish organisational wisdom; Bateson’s Learning II is not simply another name for double-loop learning; and transduction does not have a single interchangeable meaning across Beer and Simondon. A self-producing living unity also requires a more specific account than a self-organising group.

Further work

The most useful next evidence would be documented cases following these distinctions through an intervention: which distinctions a reporting system loses, what learning changed beyond the response, and how a boundary is produced and contested. That is a separate inquiry from source exposition.
