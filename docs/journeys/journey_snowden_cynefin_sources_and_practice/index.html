Snowden, Cynefin and the jobs sources can do

A route through a practitioner constellation, its core works and the different evidential roles of blog, wiki, paper, publisher and public institution.

Starts with Dave Snowden's body of work, separates its people, frameworks, tools and publications, and shows why canonical first-party sources are indispensable but not self-validating.

This is an editorial reading route. Claims about influence or dependence need the evidence recorded against the relevant connection.

Follow this journey interactively

  1. Enter through a body of work

    Snowden's expertise spans knowledge management, decision support, narrative research, naturalising sense-making, anthro-complexity and methods for acting under uncertainty.

    Read Dave Snowden

  2. Anchor the early account

    A dated primary article gives firmer historical ground than projecting today's terminology backwards.

    Read Complex Acts of Knowing

  3. Keep collaboration and dynamics visible

    The co-authored paper with Cynthia F. Kurtz connects narrative, strategy and movement among contexts.

    Read The New Dynamics of Strategy

  4. Use Cynefin as decision support

    Cynefin discriminates contexts and supports method choice; it is not a permanent taxonomy of problem types.

    Read Cynefin framework

  5. Ask where an approach applies

    Context-sensitive validity is more demanding than either universal prescription or anything-goes relativism.

    Read Bounded applicability

  6. Place the framework in the broader school

    Naturalising sense-making uses natural science as a constraint and asks what is sufficient for action.

    Read Naturalising sense-making

  7. Take human meaning and identity seriously

    Anthro-complexity warns against transferring models of particles, insects or interchangeable agents directly into human systems.

    Read Anthro-complexity

  8. Move into distributed narrative inquiry

    SenseMaker makes participant-interpreted micro-narratives available for pattern inquiry; its design and its results in any project are separate claims.

    Read SenseMaker

  9. Navigate constraints rather than promise an end state

    Estuarine Mapping treats strategy as movement through changing constraints and possibilities.

    Read Estuarine Mapping

  10. Recognise a major translation into practice

    The HBR article broadened managerial access, but it is one practice-facing account rather than the entire corpus.

    Read A Leader's Framework for Decision Making

  11. See institutional co-production

    The JRC field guide translates Cynefin into public crisis practice and keeps its collaborative authorship visible.

    Read Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis

  12. Use the blog for chronology and live argument

    The archive shows what Snowden argued and changed over time; it does not independently validate those arguments.

    Read Dave Snowden's public blog archive

  13. Use the wiki as the current semantic network

    Cynefin.io is canonical for the project's current vocabulary and connections, while page revisions and stronger sources remain necessary for contested claims.

    Read Cynefin.io
