How a whole persists without becoming rigid

Identity, adaptation, autonomy, cohesion and viability

A route through the central tension of viable organisation: changing enough without dissolving the whole.

This is an editorial reading route. Claims about influence or dependence need the evidence recorded against the relevant connection.

Follow this journey interactively

  1. Name what must remain recognisable

    Identity is continuity through change, not a demand that structures never change.

    Read Identity

  2. Respond to environmental change

    Change becomes adaptation only in relation to an environment, timescale and viability condition.

    Read Adaptation

  3. Give parts regulatory capacity

    Local units need discretion, information and resources to handle local variety.

    Read Autonomy

  4. Keep the parts a whole

    Shared identity, lateral coordination and residual escalation integrate parts without erasing their differences.

    Read Cohesion

  5. Hold the tension dynamically

    Viability is the ongoing capacity to remain a recognisable actor while operations, learning and environment change.

    Read Viability

  6. Diagnose the organisation

    The VSM offers one disciplined way to examine these functions recursively across levels.

    Read Viable System Model (VSM)
