Social complexity without the control-room fantasy

Models, interaction, power and practical judgement

A route from complexity science to a radically social account of organising and practice.

This is an editorial reading route. Claims about influence or dependence need the evidence recorded against the relevant connection.

Follow this journey interactively

  1. Take the sciences seriously

    Nonlinearity, interaction and emergence provide real explanatory resources. They do not travel into social life without translation.

    Read Complexity

  2. Put interaction at the centre

    Continuity and change arise in the interweaving of intentions, identities and power relations rather than the execution of a plan held outside the system.

    Read Complex responsive processes

  3. Meet a critical practitioner-scholar

    Mowles uses complexity to sharpen practical judgement while resisting its conversion into another managerial technology of prediction and control.

    Read Chris Mowles

  4. Distinguish the meanings of complexity

    The book treats complexity as plural and asks what changes when the subject is human social action rather than only a natural or computational system.

    Read Complexity: A Key Idea for Business and Society

  5. Return to situated practice

    Practitioner inquiry supplies cases, tensions and voices through which relational and systemic claims can be tested rather than merely repeated.

    Read Murmurations: Journal of Transformative Systemic Practice
