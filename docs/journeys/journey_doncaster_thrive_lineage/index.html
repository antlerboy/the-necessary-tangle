Readable atlas · Release 0.39

From midge swarms to municipal practice

Follow Damian Allen's self-described lineage into Doncaster Thrive, then test where public sources, practice claims and unresolved theory diverge.

This is an editorial reading route. Claims about influence or dependence need the evidence recorded against the relevant connection.

Follow this journey interactively

  1. Begin with a first-person lineage

    Damian Allen's diagram joins ecology, philosophy, learning, systems, complexity, cybernetics, design, place and relational practice. These are his claims, not a neutral canon.

    Read Damian Allen

  2. Translate lineage into a way of working

    Thrive is presented as a municipal operating approach built around neighbourhoods, relationships, assets, wellbeing and learning—not a detachable programme.

    Read Doncaster Thrive

  3. Make learning and relationship structural

    HLS explains one route from complexity to public-service practice, while efficacy still requires independent evaluation.

    Read Human Learning Systems

  4. Start with community assets—and uneven capacity

    ABCD changes who is treated as knowledgeable and capable, while retaining the need for capacity, rights and resources.

    Read Asset-Based Community Development

  5. Hold present, transition and future together

    Damian Allen describes adapting Three Horizons. Sharpe's book is public; Hodgson's exact historical role remains a first-person attribution.

    Read Three Horizons

  6. Use place networks and small bets

    Remake Learning supplies a networked route. Doncaster's parallel festival is an adaptation, not an assertion of institutional identity.

    Read Remake Learning

  7. Keep place, scale and regeneration in view

    Wahl and Andersson connect regenerative design to place; circular economy remains a neighbour, not a synonym.

    Read Regenerative cultures

  8. Return to feedback, variety and viable organization

    Beer and Ashby are explicit in Damian Allen's diagram, joining municipal practice to a deeper cybernetic route.

    Read Cybernetics

  9. Stop at the evidence boundary

    UTSI is an unpublished proto-theory. The synthesis, mathematics and framework count remain open to disproof.

    Read Unified Theory of Systems Intelligence

  10. End with an adversarial test

    Relational and kindness language must be tested against power, decisions, money, rights, accountability, resident experience and durability.

    Read Choose Kindness
