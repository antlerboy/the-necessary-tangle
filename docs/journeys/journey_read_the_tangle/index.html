How to read The Necessary Tangle

Why the line matters as much as the dot

A short orientation to typed relations, evidence and disagreement.

This is an editorial reading route. Claims about influence or dependence need the evidence recorded against the relevant connection.

Follow this journey interactively

  1. Begin with a familiar word

    Feedback seems simple until engineering, physiology, cybernetics, learning and everyday evaluation all use the same word differently.

    Read Feedback

  2. Separate a subtype

    Negative feedback is not a negative judgement. The relation type tells you that it specialises the broader feedback concept.

    Read Negative feedback

  3. Do not merge neighbours

    Homeostasis often uses feedback but names a maintained viable condition and organisation. The atlas records the confusion rather than flattening it.

    Read Homeostasis

  4. Follow evidence, not resemblance

    A logical prerequisite, a historical precursor and a later practical use are different lines and demand different evidence.

    Read Requisite variety

  5. Move from concept to practice

    The VSM page shows how concepts are used in a method without implying that every intellectual neighbour directly caused it.

    Read Viable System Model (VSM)
