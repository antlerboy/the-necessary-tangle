Emergence without magic

What complexity adds, and what the words do not explain

A route through local interaction, cross-scale pattern and the limits of command.

This is an editorial reading route. Claims about influence or dependence need the evidence recorded against the relevant connection.

Follow this journey interactively

  1. Order without central prescription

    Local interactions can produce global order. That does not remove constraints, resources, power or environment.

    Read Self-organisation

  2. A property at another scale

    Emergence names a cross-scale relation. It does not itself tell you which mechanism generated the pattern.

    Read Emergence

  3. Find amplification

    Reinforcing loops can magnify small differences and create tipping or path dependence.

    Read Positive feedback

  4. Find limits and balancing

    Growth and novelty usually meet balancing loops, resource constraints or saturation.

    Read Negative feedback

  5. Intervene in conditions

    In complex situations, intervention often changes constraints, connections and response capacity rather than delivering a predetermined outcome.

    Read Regulation
