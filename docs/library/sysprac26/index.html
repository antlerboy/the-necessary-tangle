Systemic consulting: helping without taking over

Benjamin P Taylor · 30 September 2026 · Online

The September discussion explored how offering help changes a situation. It examined the pull towards expertise or compliant service, the client behind the client, and the continuing negotiation of responsibility. Internal consulting brings additional relationships: a line manager, a sponsor, and people who can authorise or obstruct the work. Authenticity and contracting for learning remained practical questions.

The accompanying field guide develops Benjamin’s consulting teaching from 2022 to 2026, drawing particularly on Peter Block and Barry Oshry. It offers contracting questions, practice situations, a learning contract, and a one-page practice map. Readers can use these resources before a conversation, with a colleague, or through an assignment.

Making relational, place-based public services ordinary

Benjamin P Taylor · 8 October 2026 · Online

The workshop examined two connected tasks: improving what happens in a person’s life, and changing the conditions that allow useful practice to endure. Its public resource follows a fictional situation through service contact, records, measures, management decisions, and the next encounter. What survives each translation, what disappears, and who has to join things up?

The discussion addressed information that is recorded without changing the response, case closure without evidence that a need is resolved, and the gap between knowing what should change and having authority to change it. The developed resource includes a working tool, an infographic, and a facilitator guide for designing a learning loop and a bounded institutional experiment.
