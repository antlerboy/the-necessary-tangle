Leading Transformation: two dated learning routes

Leading Transformation is the Public Service Transformation Academy’s programme. RedQuadrant’s Tool Shed is a supported practitioner learning and application offer using related content. The original 24-module architecture is retained here alongside the revised route agreed on 6 September 2026.

Leading Transformation programme · RedQuadrant Tool Shed

Revised route: 6 September 2026

The five worlds are perspectives with cross-links: leadership and political; citizen; service; management; learning and change. Five Core Practices recur throughout. Live work begins in module 3, service prototyping in module 12, and the Four Dynamics are introduced in module 4 and applied in module 18. The final simulation requires a substantial delivery block and hundred-day follow-through.

ModuleFocus and worldPractice
1Transformation and the five worlds
Leadership and political		Read a live transformation challenge through leadership, citizens, services, management, and learning.
2Purpose, outcomes, and useful measures
Leadership and political		Agree purpose, distinguish outputs from outcomes, and choose measures that help people act.
3Your practice as a transformation agent
Learning and change		Contract for useful work, examine your own contribution, and start a live practice challenge.
4Power, responsibility, and distributed leadership
Leadership and political		Work with power, accountability, and distributed leadership; introduce the Four Dynamics.
5Honest, productive conversations
Learning and change		Practise listening, productive challenge, difficult conversations, and clear agreements.
6Systems inquiry, mapping, and method choice
Learning and change		Frame a useful inquiry, map different perspectives, and choose methods for the situation.
7Citizen insight and behaviour in context
Citizen		Gather citizen insight and examine behaviour in its social and practical context.
8Strengths, community, and co-production
Citizen		Work with strengths, community assets, co-production, and Positive Deviance.
9Demand, access, and prevention
Service		Understand demand, access, avoidable work, and opportunities for prevention.
10Relational public services
Citizen		Organise around people’s lives, relationships, and needs that cross service boundaries.
11Designing services and the work behind them
Service		Connect the citizen’s experience with service design, processes, and the work behind them.
12Prototyping and learning through action
Learning and change		Test service ideas through prototypes and small experiments, then use what happens to improve them.
13Lean, flow, and operational learning
Service		Apply lean, flow, A3 inquiry, and operational tools with respect for the people doing the work.
14Resource Optimisation and choices about work
Management		Make effort and capacity visible, and use Resource Optimisation and Seven Ways to examine choices.
15Costs, benefits, and business cases
Management		Connect activity, costs, benefits, and options in a defensible business case.
16Digital, data, and AI judgement
Service		Judge digital, data, and AI proposals against service purpose, evidence, and human responsibility.
17Understanding and designing viable organisations
Management		Use the Viable System Model to examine autonomy, coordination, and organisational viability.
18The Four Dynamics, roles, and organisation design
Management		Apply the Four Dynamics to roles, relationships, authority, and organisation design.
19Culture, change, and meeting the other
Management		Work with culture, assumptions, identity, and encounters with people who see things differently.
20Convening across communities and organisations
Citizen		Build the relationships and conditions for action across communities and organisations.
21Strategy, commissioning, and choices about provision
Leadership and political		Use Patterns of Strategy, commissioning, and provision choices to connect intent with action.
22Facilitating groups and designing interventions
Learning and change		Choose and facilitate group processes, and design interventions that fit their setting.
23Delivering, embedding, and evaluating change
Learning and change		Plan delivery, use Appreciative Inquiry and Breaking the Shell, and evaluate and embed change.
24Integration, simulation, and the next hundred days
Learning and change		Bring the repertoire together through simulation and a substantial hundred-day follow-through.
