The link contract

Preservation is not endorsement. A source link remains a claim made by that map. It enters the canonical Tangle only after identity, meaning and evidence have been checked separately.

The *Map of Systemic Evolution* was already present in Benjamin P Taylor’s SysCoI collection of prior maps and histories and registered in The Necessary Tangle. What remains deliberately unfinished

Other registered maps and bodies of knowledge remain in the comparator programme. Where a machine-readable link set is available and use is permitted, it should be retained in full under this contract. Where only an image or source page exists, the project preserves the complete source route and does not invent endpoints or meanings.
