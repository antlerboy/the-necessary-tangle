Reading the inheritance: map review, October 2026

The inheritance contains different kinds of map: historical arguments, visual teaching accounts, explicit source networks, and bibliometric signals. A common drawing style is not evidence that their lines mean the same thing.

This review identifies the public works and the available source texts. Text search did not establish a complete inventory of image-only files. Private storage details and source files are not republished.
