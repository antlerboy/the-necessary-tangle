Responsibility and maintenance

Benjamin P Taylor is the curator and remains responsible for editorial acceptance and publication. Reviewer names and decisions belong on the records they have reviewed. The roles below describe responsibilities, rather than appointments or publishing permissions.

Contribute a bounded review

Choose one entry, source passage, or connection. Give its URL, the exact statement, what should change, the evidence and locator, and any relevant limits or competing interpretation. A proposal does not grant publishing access.

Send a correction or review proposal.

What maintenance involves

The static entry and journey pages are generated from the existing public projection. They do not form a second evidence store. Corrections belong in the maintained atlas records and their release generator.

Sources, recorded status, passage locators, interactive routes, and calendar subscriptions must survive a release. A failed source check is a maintenance problem, not permission to remove uncertainty or invent a replacement.

Maintenance responsibilities

  • Curator: acceptance, wording, disputed claims, and release decisions.
  • Contributors: a specific attributed proposal, source, and locator.
  • Maintainers: generation, links, sitemap, accessibility, downloads, and preservation of existing interactive behaviour.
  • Automated checks: defects and consistency only; they cannot confer truth, competence, or editorial acceptance.

Reuse

Entry downloads contain existing original atlas records, source metadata, and locators. They exclude source documents and evidence excerpts. Retain attribution, scope, release, and review status; linked third-party works keep their own terms.
