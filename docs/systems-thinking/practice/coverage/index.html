Standards coverage and limits

Editorial mapping for practice, not SCiO endorsement, accreditation or an end-point assessment judgement. The portfolio distinguishes training/case-study application from work in organisations. These fictional exercises remain in that learning category.

Sources: Public self-assessment portfolio, v11 February 2025 and ST0787 version 1.2, effective 20 February 2025. Checked 7 September 2026. This mapping is editorial, not a replacement for either standard.

Apprenticeship K2 requires working knowledge of at least three approaches, including at least two of CSH, SSM, System Dynamics and VSM. This wider library offers choice; it does not make all thirteen compulsory.

SCiO approaches

Framework rowFree practiceDepth and limit
Confrontation Analysis (CONAN)Confrontation Analysis: separate positions, options and credibilityAn introductory confrontation-analysis rehearsal, not a full formal classification of dilemmas or a prediction of behaviour. Consent, new options and changing preferences remain possible.
Critical System Heuristics (CSH: Ulrich, Reynolds)Critical Systems Heuristics: inspect all twelve boundary categoriesThis exercise checks the completeness and consistency of boundary reasoning. It does not supply a morally unique answer or pretend that the practitioner represents every affected person.
INFORMED Group Dynamics (Marguet)INFORMED Group Dynamics: investigate the distinctions behind a decisionAn original introductory rehearsal using the publicly described INFORMED framing and personal-construct orientation. It is not Marguet's complete taught protocol, a personality assessment, or a validated repertory-grid diagnosis. Use supervised teaching for the fuller approach.
Interactive Management (Warfield & Christakis)Interactive Management: construct a small interpretive structural modelThis rehearses one modelling component of Interactive Management. It does not reproduce its full facilitated group process or establish group agreement. The external handbook's free-download availability is uncertain, so the complete case is supplied here.
Interactive planning (Ackoff)Interactive planning: design the service you would choose nowA small rehearsal of Ackoff's interactive planning and idealised design. It is not an invitation to ignore present feasibility, affected people or implementation.
Mosaic Transformation (Hoverstadt), Bubble StrategyMosaic transformation and bubble strategy: sequence changes you can sustainAn original small rehearsal of published sequencing ideas. It does not reproduce a proprietary training exercise or claim that a protected bubble is a complete Mosaic transformation.
Multi-methodology (Rosenhead, Jackson, Ison)Multi-methodology: justify the move from one method to anotherA basic methodological design exercise. There is no universal correct sequence. The checks concern fit, assumptions and transitions, not allegiance to one classification of methods.
Patterns of Strategy (Hoverstadt & Loh)Patterns of Strategy: model dependence, fit and timingAn original introductory relationship exercise. It does not reproduce the book's pattern catalogue or claim that one numerical ratio captures strategic power.
Socio-Technical Systems (Trist)Socio-Technical Systems: redesign the work and technology togetherA basic primary-work-system exercise informed by the socio-technical tradition. It is not a complete participative design process or a substitute for workers' and users' involvement.
Soft Systems Methodology (SSM)Soft Systems Methodology: build a model that follows its purposeA compact SSM rehearsal. The stages organise learning here; they are not a claim that SSM is a mandatory linear project sequence. An activity model is an inquiry device, not a description of what the organisation really is.
Syntegration/SyntegrityTMSyntegration: test the communication structure before the eventA paper exercise about the classic structure, not delivery of a licensed or full Team Syntegrity event. It does not replace facilitation, topic formation, role allocation or the complete protocol. Other developed formats exist.
System Dynamics (Forrester)System Dynamics: build, calculate and challenge a queue modelAn original discrete-time stock-flow rehearsal with a deliberately simple boundary. It is not a prediction of a real service and does not validate a model merely because it runs.
Viable System Model (VSM)Viable System Model: diagnose functions and channelsA basic organisational diagnosis. Functions can be distributed or combined in people and groups. The exercise does not establish that every multi-agency network has a single legitimate metasystem.

Systemic thinking & systems conceptsSystems concepts: make your boundary inspectableA first rehearsal of boundaries, relationships, perspectives, feedback and emergence. It is not a complete treatment of systems philosophy or complexity science.
Systems LawsSystems laws: test variety, feedback and local optimisationBasic examples of requisite variety, feedback delay and whole-system effects. The SCiO row is broader; this page does not claim to cover every law in a systems-laws course.

Its resource list also cites Yi Lin's systemic yoyo; this exercise does not teach or validate that distinct formal model. The terminology and intended depth should be checked with your tutor.

Systemic intervention and inquiry

The third section of the SCiO portfolio mixes systemic intervention practices with generic professional techniques. The ten additional pages provide basic rehearsals for the explicitly systemic and closely related inquiry, learning, conversation, service and evaluation work named in their headings. They are not a claim to teach every professional technique in that section.

  • Strategic Options Development and Analysis (SODA): SODA: build a means-ends map without erasing disagreement
  • Fractal Enterprise Model & Capabilities (Bider): Fractal Enterprise Model: alternate processes and the assets they depend on
  • Critical Social Learning Systems; Action learning; Action research; Appreciative Enquiry: Critical social learning: build a learning relationship across boundaries
  • Deming; Vanguard Method (Seddon); Lean & Six-Sigma: Service systems: follow demand and distinguish activity from resolution
  • Stakeholder analysis; Influence mapping; Interview technique; Listening & multiple perspectives; Large group engagement processes: Participative inquiry: test who your evidence leaves out
  • Systemic inquiry and intervention design: futures, uncertainty and complexity: Futures and probes: test robustness without pretending to predict
  • Multi-agency intervention; Values mapping and integration; Managing deflection / resistance & challenge: Ethics and power: make an intervention's authority and effects explicit
  • Action research; Scientific Theory & Evidence; Learning design & learning conversations: Evaluation and reflexivity: test the claim you are tempted to make
  • Constellations (Systemic): Systemic constellations: use a spatial representation as a hypothesis
  • Conversation mapping; Ladder of inference; Productive conversations; Detecting & Managing undiscussables: Systemic conversations: expose inference and repair a broken commitment

What this pack does not supply

  • Generic presentation, public-speaking, selling, training-design and coaching qualifications are not supplied by this pack.
  • NLP, transactional analysis and other separately trained practices are not relabelled as systems methods or certified here.
  • Advanced statistics, full Six Sigma, specialist sector law, safeguarding and regulatory compliance need their own study and contextual assessment.
  • Real facilitation, collaboration, implementation, resilience and professional conduct cannot be established by a solo practice page.
  • The Systems Laws and Transposing Theory rows receive explicit introductory exercises, not a claim of exhaustive theoretical coverage. INFORMED, Interactive Management and Syntegration pages state which part of their fuller process is rehearsed.
