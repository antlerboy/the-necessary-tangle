Autopoiesis

Self-production of a unity through a recursively constituted network of component production.

Review status

Profile: research pass needs editor. Entry: research pass needs editor.

Biological self-production and operational discretion are compared explicitly. This source-located distinction remains a candidate for editorial acceptance.

Summary

The biological account includes the production of a boundary that enables the processes producing it. Material turnover is compatible with the persistence of this organisation. The paper's biological use of autonomy concerns self-production of a unity. It doesn't establish the decision rights of its parts.

Implications for practice

A boundary drawn by an analyst and a boundary produced by a living system make different claims.

Key distinctions

  • Self-production versus reproduction
  • Organisation versus particular components
  • Organisational closure versus material isolation
  • Biological self-production versus managerial discretion

Connections to practice

  • Before applying the term, identify components, production processes, and boundary formation.

Common confusions

  • Any durable feedback loop is autopoietic.
  • Calling an institution living establishes biological autopoiesis.

Further checks

  • Social applications need an explicit account of what is being produced.
  • Any organisational application must state its components, production processes, unit of analysis, and decision rights separately.
