Understanding Systems: a twelve-module reading guide

David Ing’s complete 2026 Understanding Systems pro forma is now available to read. It connects systems inquiry, design, visualisation, and collective learning across twelve modules.

Published pro forma for discussion; not evidence that every suggestion entered the launched syllabus. The pro forma is published under CC BY by David Ing. The headings, short guides, and inquiry prompts below are editorial aids. Links identify works in the pro forma; inclusion does not mean the atlas has read every linked work.
