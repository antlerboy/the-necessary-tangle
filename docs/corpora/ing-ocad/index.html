One explicit comparison

Reynolds and Holwell’s introductory abstract compares SD, VSM, SODA, SSM and CSH. Its rationale concerns change and uncertainty, constructivist use, and combinations of methods. Read the chapter abstract and access options. This is abstract-level evidence, not a claim to have read the subscription chapter.

How to read the connections

Selection for teaching, authorship, a conceptual comparison and historical influence are different statements. The map records which kind is being made. A course’s order is not evidence that one author influenced the next.
