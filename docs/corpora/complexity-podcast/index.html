Bounded source corpus

The Complexity Podcast

The complete public run of the Santa Fe Institute's COMPLEXITY podcast is now registered as a transcript-backed source corpus: 119 feed items from 2019 to 2024.

What changed in 0.19

The atlas now has a developed entry for the series, three explicit source records and a corpus register. The official SFI archive states that full transcripts are available for every episode at Simplecast. Registration makes the material findable and governable; it does not pretend that every transcript has been deeply interpreted.

What it does not prove

A guest appearance does not prove agreement, priority, truth, field-wide importance or influence. Co-occurrence in a transcript is not a useful graph relation. Episode-level claims enter the atlas only with a clear relation, source locator, scope and review status.
