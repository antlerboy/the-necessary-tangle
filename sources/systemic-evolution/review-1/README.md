## Published lineage

The URANOS page describes the graph as:

- originated in 1996 by Eric Schwarz;
- extended in 1998 with material from Will Durant's *The Story of Philosophy*;
- elaborated in 2000–01 for the International Institute for General Systems
  Studies;
- extended in 2016 by Benjamin Hadorn.

The permission response requires visible attribution to Eric Schwarz, Benjamin
Hadorn and Beat Hirsbrunner. The Necessary Tangle also retains the full lineage
above, and separately credits Benjamin P Taylor for the 2019 SysCoI
registration, permission work and editorial reconciliation, and Nigel Williams
for the deterministic extraction and first comparator implementation.

## Meaning of the source links

The source page says the directed edges illustrate major influences between
topics. Individual edges in the GraphML have no separate citation, description
or finer relation label. The comparator therefore preserves every line as a
source-reported major influence and does not treat it as independently verified
or as a canonical Tangle relation.
