# Permission record: Map of Systemic Evolution

Status: **permission granted; derivative publication subject to prior review**

Permission received: **26 August 2026**

Grantor: **Benjamin Hadorn**

Project: **The Necessary Tangle**

Public discussion record:
<https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/2#issuecomment-5424930678>

## Scope requested and accepted

Benjamin P Taylor asked for permission to:

- retain and publish the 2016 GraphML in the public project repository;
- parse and publish its labels, directed structure and relevant metadata as
  machine-readable comparator data;
- reproduce screenshots and interactive visualisations;
- annotate, correct and extend it, including descriptions and evidence for
  individual relationships;
- publish an enriched derivative GraphML and return it to the maintainers; and
- incorporate selected, independently checked material into The Necessary
  Tangle.

Benjamin Hadorn confirmed that he was happy to grant permission for that scope
and those activities, subject to both conditions below.

## Binding conditions

1. **Full attribution.** Clearly attribute the authorship of the original
   material and its extensions, including Eric Schwarz, Benjamin Hadorn and
   Beat Hirsbrunner.
2. **Review of modifications.** Provide every adapted, extended or modified
   version of the graph or its data to the maintainers for review and
   verification before publication.

Hadorn also confirmed that the other parties do not need to be approached
separately for this complete version provided these conditions are respected.

## Licence marking

The request proposed CC BY-SA 4.0 for the GraphML and authorised derivatives.
The reply permits sharing derivatives under the stated conditions but does not
itself use the words “licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0”. The project therefore does
not describe the original graph or its arrangement as generally CC-licensed.

The Necessary Tangle's original annotations and code remain under their stated
project licences. Any file that combines those additions with source graph
structure is marked as an authorised project-specific derivative and carries
the attribution and pre-publication-review conditions above.

## Operational consequence

The unmodified source may be preserved in the review package. The enriched
GraphML, comparator changes and revised interactive rendering must remain
unpublished until Hadorn/Hirsbrunner have reviewed the exact checksummed
derivative package. A later change to graph data requires a new review.
