About this view

Inspired by SCiO’s SysBoK concept model and its Precedents and Dependent Derivatives view. The records here belong to The Necessary Tangle. SCiO describes its own model as incomplete and work in progress.

Only relationships classified as prerequisite claims enter the predecessor/dependent columns. The other-relationships mode includes citation, collaboration, authorship, influence, and collection structure with their recorded meanings. Proposals and disputes keep their review labels.
