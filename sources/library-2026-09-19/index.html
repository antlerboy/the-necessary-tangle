What the connections mean

An author or presenter credit records a named title-page credit, including co-authorship. A teaching account identifies a method presented at a cited page. An identity link points to the same named method in the atlas. A text mention locates a phrase in a source. A title match is a discovery aid based on the catalogue title.

Text and title matches do not establish agreement, influence, authorship, or logical dependence. A source may discuss a concept to criticise it. The relationship needs reading in context before a stronger claim is made.

Prepared with AI assistance. Independent specialist review is not recorded. Original works retain their own rights; this register does not republish the private research files.
