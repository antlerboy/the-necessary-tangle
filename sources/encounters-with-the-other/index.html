About this adaptation

The animation follows the argument of the supplied book. It uses moving relationship diagrams and condensed wording. Power is paired with difference and separateness; love with commonality and connectedness. Neither is treated as a personality type.

The historical catalogue is condensed. Its casualty estimates are omitted, and its dated accounts are not presented as current reporting. The final two questions are additions for this adaptation. The transcript gives the source page for every scene.

The narrator is a synthetic Piper voice. This is an adaptation prepared for review, not a recording of Barry or a claim of his approval. Book: Encounters with the Other, Barry Oshry, Triarchy Press, 2026 free edition. ISBN 978-1-911193-70-8. Copyright Barry Oshry, 2024 and 2026.
