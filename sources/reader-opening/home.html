The museum alarm

A museum’s duty officer gets the same warning when its store is too hot and when it’s too cold. Heating and cooling are both available. Which switch should she press?

Read the example and its sources

W. Ross Ashby asked what a regulator must distinguish. He and Roger Conant later showed how its choice of action can embody a model. Stafford Beer’s account of communication channels helps explain what happened to the temperature reading on its way to the officer.
