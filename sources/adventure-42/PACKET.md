# Release 0.42: an adventure through the Tangle

Authorised by Benjamin P Taylor on 15 November 2026. Deliver `/42/`: an original,
branching, persistent text adventure inspired by the CP/M edition of Douglas
Adams and Steve Meretzky's Infocom Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. Scope is the
whole field of systems | cybernetics | complexity, preserving mathematical,
biological, technical, artistic, historical, philosophical, and practical
differences. Do not narrow this to public-service management or turn each author
into a simple lesson.

## Maintained inputs and output

`worlds.json` holds original fictional places, objects, and parser suggestions.
`scripts/build_adventure_42.py` checks their atlas anchors, projects every
canonical public record and its source-labelled connections from
`data/public-data.json`, and builds `docs/42/`. The engine, stylesheet, and page
template are maintained here. Scientific apparatus is explicitly finite and
original: a discrete feedback toy, rule 110 with fixed empty boundaries, a
Lorenz/RK4 numerical illustration, and an exact Koch-construction calculation.
The fiction is not scientific evidence or historical dialogue.

The existing graph research release remains 0.41. The new reader release is
0.42; `docs/42/manifest.json` and `deployment.json.reader_release` distinguish
these. Do not alter canonical nodes, relationships, profiles, acceptance status,
or protected third-party comparator packages for this interface release.

puzzles and numerical mechanisms, reachable places, genuine atlas/source links,

generators together. Publish under AGENTS.md standing authority after checks.
