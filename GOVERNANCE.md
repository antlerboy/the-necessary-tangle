# Governance

## Founding curator

[Benjamin P Taylor](https://www.antlerboy.com/) is the founding curator of The Necessary Tangle and is accountable for its published releases.

Curatorship means responsibility for the current selection, wording, sources, boundaries and decisions. It does not imply sole creation of the knowledge, histories or practices represented here.

## Curatorial principles

1. **The public name is The Necessary Tangle.**
2. **Every connection must say what it means.** ‘Related to’ is not an adequate published relationship.
3. **Different evidence supports different statements.** Citation, contact, collaboration, teaching, influence, precedence and logical dependence are not interchangeable.
4. **Status and uncertainty remain visible.** Provisional, disputed and superseded statements are not presented as settled facts.
5. **Public releases use public-safe material.** Public URLs are used where available; legitimate published sources without open copies are marked ‘No public link’.
6. **Changes are reviewable and reversible.** Issues, pull requests, commits and releases preserve the decision trail.
7. **Automation remains human-accountable.** Agent-assisted work has a named human sponsor and no independent merge authority.

## Decision process

Substantive changes should have a precise statement, named entities, a controlled connection type where relevant, a scope note, a source and locator, and a recorded curatorial outcome.

The curator may accept, revise, defer, mark as disputed or decline a proposal. Reasons should be recorded where the decision is not obvious.

## Participation and access

The project distinguishes Reader, Proposer, Research contributor, Reviewer, Maintainer and Curator roles. Their meaning and actual GitHub limits are set out in [documentation/participation-and-access.md](documentation/participation-and-access.md).

The repository currently belongs to a personal GitHub account, so the finer project roles are enforced through process rather than granular repository permissions. A move to a GitHub organisation should be considered when the real review load and contributor group justify it.

## Agent-assisted work

Agents and LLMs do not become members. They act under a named human sponsor who discloses the tool, checks sources and diffs, and remains responsible. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## Future governance

A wider curatorial and review group should follow the actual variety of the work: subject knowledge, archive work, evidence review, data maintenance, software and public explanation. The structure should grow from real contribution and disagreement rather than being invented in advance.
