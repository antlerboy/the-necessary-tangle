# Governance

## Founding editor

Benjamin P Taylor is the creator and founding editor of The Necessary Tangle and is accountable for the published releases.

## Editorial principles

The project follows six rules:

1. **The public name is The Necessary Tangle.** It is not shortened in headings, metadata or releases.
2. **Every connection must say what it means.** ‘Related to’ is not an adequate published relationship.
3. **Different evidence supports different claims.** Citation, contact, collaboration, teaching, influence, precedence and logical dependence are not interchangeable.
4. **Status and uncertainty remain visible.** Provisional, disputed and superseded claims are not presented as settled facts.
5. **Public releases use public-safe material.** Public URLs are used where available; legitimate published sources without open copies are marked ‘No public link’.
6. **Changes are reviewable and reversible.** Issues, pull requests, commits and releases preserve the decision trail.

## Decision process

Substantive changes should have:

- a precise statement;
- named entities and a controlled connection type where relevant;
- a scope note;
- a source and locator;
- a recorded editorial outcome.

The founding editor may accept, revise, defer, mark as disputed or decline a proposal. Reasons should be recorded where the decision is not obvious.

## Future governance

The project is expected to develop a wider editorial group once the public alpha has generated enough real contributions to show what roles and review capacity are needed. Governance should follow the actual variety of the work rather than inventing a committee before there is a functioning community to govern.
