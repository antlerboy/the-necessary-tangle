# Governance

## Founding curator

[Benjamin P Taylor](https://www.antlerboy.com/) is the founding curator of The Necessary Tangle and is accountable for its published releases.

Curatorship means responsibility for the current selection, wording, sources, boundaries and editorial decisions. It does not imply sole creation of the knowledge, histories or practices represented here.

## Editorial principles

The project follows six rules:

1. **The public name is The Necessary Tangle.** It is not shortened in headings, metadata or releases.
2. **Every connection must say what it means.** ‘Related to’ is not an adequate published relationship.
3. **Different evidence supports different statements.** Citation, contact, collaboration, teaching, influence, precedence and logical dependence are not interchangeable.
4. **Status and uncertainty remain visible.** Provisional, disputed and superseded statements are not presented as settled facts.
5. **Public releases use public-safe material.** Public URLs are used where available; legitimate published sources without open copies are marked ‘No public link’.
6. **Changes are reviewable and reversible.** Issues, pull requests, commits and releases preserve the decision trail.

## Decision process

Substantive changes should have:

- a precise statement;
- named entities and a controlled connection type where relevant;
- a scope note;
- a source and locator;
- a recorded curatorial outcome.

The founding curator may accept, revise, defer, mark as disputed or decline a proposal. Reasons should be recorded where the decision is not obvious.

## Future governance

The project should develop a wider editorial group once public use has exposed the actual review work, knowledge domains and capacity required. Governance should follow the variety of the work rather than inventing a committee before there is a functioning community to govern.
