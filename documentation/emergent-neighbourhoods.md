# Observed neighbourhoods

Release `0.7-constellations-alpha` publishes a first diagnostic of structure already present in the evidence graph. It does not declare a new taxonomy of the field.

## Method

The analysis uses canonical public entries other than publications, corpora, sources and events. It includes typed conceptual, historical, influence, practice, human, identity and contestation connections. Documentary, evidential, classification, legacy and unresolved associations are excluded from the clustering calculation.

The script first reports connected components. Within connected material it applies a deterministic greedy modularity procedure. Names are then assigned from central entries and the language of the current records. The result is reproducible for this release.

## Current result

The present graph contains 203 entries in the analytic population, but 129 are isolated under these rules. The six named neighbourhoods are:

1. feedback, control and learning;
2. recursion, computation and self-reference;
3. viability, variety and organisation;
4. boundaries, information and systemic intervention;
5. observation, emergence and self-organisation;
6. metasystem-transition theory and its formulators.

The first five are recognisable conceptual-practice knots. The sixth is a small Principia Cybernetica cluster introduced in this release.

## What this means

The graph currently has a few coherent neighbourhoods and a large weakly connected periphery. The periphery is mainly a research finding about the atlas: many entries have orientation text but insufficient reviewed connections. It should not be reified as hundreds of separate schools.

The neighbourhoods are observer-dependent products of:

- what the curator has included;
- which relationships have been researched and typed;
- the current evidence threshold;
- the excluded relation families;
- the clustering rule;
- the names applied after calculation.

They are useful because they expose the consequences of those choices. They are not natural kinds waiting patiently inside the data.

## Next checks

Later releases should compare several clustering methods, test sensitivity to relation weighting, publish unnamed results before interpretation, and track how corpus passes change isolation and neighbourhood membership.
