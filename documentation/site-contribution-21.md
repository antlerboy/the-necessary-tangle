# Site contribution #21: viability, fitness and natural drift

## Origin

This contribution was submitted through the public site by Ivo Velitchkov (`kvistgaard`) and became GitHub issue #21.

## Proposed distinction

The contribution challenges a loose equation between viability and fitness. In the autopoietic and enactive tradition, an organism need not be an optimising winner in a universal contest. It must remain viable within its history of structural coupling. ‘Natural drift’ directs attention towards conservation of organisation and the contingent pathways through which viable lineages continue, rather than treating evolution as the progressive selection of an abstractly ‘fittest’ type.

## Curatorial treatment

The release:

- adds the distinction to the viability entry;
- records natural drift as a linked concept rather than a synonym for natural selection;
- attributes the originating correction to issue #21;
- uses public primary and scholarly sources before promoting the wording into the public graph;
- does not claim that natural drift replaces every use of selection theory or that ‘fitness’ has only one technical meaning.

The point is to prevent a familiar slogan from swallowing several distinct explanatory traditions.
