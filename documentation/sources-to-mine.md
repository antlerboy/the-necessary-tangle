# Sources to mine

This is a live research queue, not a list of sources whose contents have already been accepted into the atlas. Each item states its use and its limits.

## Chris Mowles and the Complexity and Management community

Status: `active_first_pass`

Public starting point: https://chrismowles.substack.com/p/resources-on-the-complexity-of-social

Use: A route into radically social accounts of complexity, complex responsive processes, practical judgement, power and organisational life.

Caution: Author-curated discovery material. Follow through to books, articles, repositories and rival accounts before asserting genealogy or consensus.

Next: Inventory the resource guide and develop the Mowles, Stacey, Griffin, Shaw and Hertfordshire community constellation.

## Murmurations: Journal of Transformative Systemic Practice

Status: `registered_first_pass`

Public starting point: https://murmurations.cloud/index.php/pub

Use: Open-access practitioner research on relational, systemic, social-constructionist and reflexive practice across therapy, organisations, leadership and community work.

Caution: The journal's scope is adjacent to, not identical with, systems science or complexity science. Article-level relevance must be judged rather than assumed.

Next: Index titles, abstracts, authors, DOI records and revival-paper conversations; admit only materially relevant items.

## Monoskop

Status: `registered`

Public starting point: https://monoskop.org/

Use: Discovery corpus for cybernetics, systems art, media theory, intellectual history, people and bibliographies.

Caution: A discovery source, not automatic evidence for claims on linked pages.

Next: Begin with high-yield people and movements, then replace discovery links with primary or scholarly sources.

## Systems Community of Inquiry archive

Status: `registered`

Public starting point: https://stream.syscoi.com/

Use: Evidence of circulation, discussion, interpretation and practitioner attention.

Caution: Posting or discussion does not prove priority, influence or acceptance.

Next: Inventory posts and outbound sources, preserving chronology and differentiating curation from lineage.

## Preserved model.report archive

Status: `registered`

Public starting point: https://syscoi.com/model.report/model.report/newest.html

Use: Historical practitioner-community archive and source-discovery trail.

Caution: The archive is incomplete and static; missing context and deleted functionality must remain visible.

Next: Recover chronology, participants, outbound links and conversations where the archive permits it.

## W. Ross Ashby Digital Archive

Status: `canonical_source`

Public starting point: https://ashby.info/

Use: Primary records, chronology, notebooks and correspondence for Ashby and early cybernetics.

Caution: An archive enables source discovery; interpretation still needs locators and historical context.

Next: Deepen Ashby, requisite variety, homeostasis, ultrastability and good-regulator lineages.

## American Society for Cybernetics library

Status: `canonical_source`

Public starting point: https://asc-cybernetics.org/

Use: Professional archive and discovery point for first- and second-order cybernetics.

Caution: Institutional curation is evidence of field memory, not a neutral or complete canon.

Next: Register public papers, talks, biographies and conference records item by item.

## International Society for the Systems Sciences history and World of Systems

Status: `to_scope`

Public starting point: https://www.isss.org/

Use: Institutional history, systems-science lineages, proceedings and public educational material.

Caution: Society histories may privilege their own institutional continuity and terminology.

Next: Identify stable public history, proceedings and concept pages; compare with rival institutional accounts.

## IFSR Conversations and publications

Status: `to_scope`

Public starting point: https://www.ifsr.org/

Use: Cross-tradition conversations, proceedings and systems research networks.

Caution: Conversation outputs require participant, date and document-level provenance.

Next: Inventory public conversations and map documented collaborations and conceptual disputes.

## System Dynamics Society bibliography and conference proceedings

Status: `to_scope`

Public starting point: https://systemdynamics.org/

Use: Primary institutional source for system dynamics methods, history, teaching and applications.

Caution: Institutional sources should be paired with critical histories and evaluations of practice.

Next: Develop Forrester, modelling practice, policy use and methodological criticism.

## Santa Fe Institute and Complexity Explorer

Status: `registered`

Public starting point: https://www.santafe.edu/

Use: Complexity-science research, people, publications, courses and institutional history.

Caution: SFI is a central institution, not the whole field; its selections and vocabulary shape what becomes visible.

Next: Replace the current table-of-contents monoculture with paper-level and author-level sources.

## New England Complex Systems Institute

Status: `to_scope`

Public starting point: https://necsi.edu/

Use: Complex-systems research, education, applications and public explanations.

Caution: Public explainers vary in evidential depth; use original papers for strong claims.

Next: Identify distinctive concepts, methods and application lineages not already covered by SFI sources.
