# Coverage programme

The public alpha is broad but uneven. A mapped item may have a responsible orientation paragraph without yet having a full concept genealogy, primary-source trail, practice account or reviewed human lineage. The work below turns the largest known gaps into bounded, inspectable programmes.

Release 0.7 adds a first grouping pass. Its main result is not a tidy taxonomy but the discovery that practice skills, tools, methods and laws are far less connected than the conceptual core. The ordering below therefore favours sourced bridges over more isolated entries.

## 1. Foundational Papers in Complexity Science

Issue: https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/3

Release 0.8 completes the first official-table-of-contents inventory: all 89 historical papers, 119 author forms (107 newly represented people and 12 reused existing entries), four collection volumes, and typed authorship and collection-structure records are now public.

This is bibliographic breadth, not completion. The next work is to trace each paper to an original or authoritative public record where possible, distinguish the collection editors' framing from the originals, summarise each paper, map the concepts and arguments it supports, and record serious counter-accounts. The collection is a major corpus, not a neutral or exhaustive canon.

## 2. Relevant Monoskop material

Issue: https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/4

Monoskop contains substantial material around people, publications, media theory, systems art, cybernetics and adjacent traditions. The project will identify materially relevant pages and bibliographies, use Monoskop as a public discovery source, follow links to stronger originals where possible, and record admissions, exclusions and deferrals.

Completion means a public corpus guide explaining what was searched, what entered the atlas, what did not, and why. It does not mean copying Monoskop or importing every page containing the word ‘system’.

## 3. SysCoI and model.report archives

Issue: https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/5

The Systems Community of Inquiry and preserved model.report archive are evidence of circulation, curation, interpretation, discussion and community participation. They are not automatic proof of priority, teaching or intellectual influence.

The work will inventory relevant public posts, tags, authors, comments and outbound sources; recover chronology where possible; and create repeatable ingestion and de-duplication rules. Stronger primary and scholarly sources will replace discovery links where available.

## 4. Prior maps and bodies of knowledge

Issue: https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/6

The project will compare earlier maps, bodies of knowledge, genealogies and reading lists, including Brian Castellani's map of the complexity sciences and Benjamin P Taylor's public critique.

Each comparator will be assessed for purpose, audience, boundary, categories, meaning of lines, evidence, strengths, omissions and reifications. The result must include where The Necessary Tangle performs worse, not just where it claims to improve on predecessors.

## 5. Practitioner influence constellations

Issue: https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/7

This develops David Ing's practitioner-centred provocation. Teaching, mentoring, supervision, collaboration, correspondence, shared institutions, event participation, citation, practical adoption and self-declared influence require separate relationships and evidence thresholds.

Completion means several gold-standard practitioner constellations and a readable ‘rock family tree’ view generated from the same evidence graph, including uncertainty, competing accounts and independent convergence.

## 6. Company-knowledge discovery

Issue: https://github.com/antlerboy/the-necessary-tangle/issues/8

Relevant SharePoint and company knowledge can reveal prior SysBoK material, presentations, bibliographies, comparators and project decisions. It is a private discovery source, not public evidence by default.

A bounded first private discovery pass is complete. It found substantial working collections around laws and principles, method selection, differentiation and integration, power and convening, public-service practice and bibliographies. No private URL or extract has entered the public repository. The result is a public replacement queue rather than imported content. See [company-knowledge-discovery-first-pass.md](company-knowledge-discovery-first-pass.md).

Useful discoveries must be replaced by legitimate public evidence wherever possible. Where no open copy exists, the public atlas may carry a full bibliographic or archive citation marked ‘No public link’. Client-confidential, personal, contractual and operational material is excluded.

## 7. Principia Cybernetica

First-pass note: [principia-cybernetica-first-pass.md](principia-cybernetica-first-pass.md)

Release 0.7 adds the project, web, three central contributors, evolutionary cybernetics, metasystem transition, semantic network and global brain, with typed and sourced relations.

The systematic pass must inventory in-scope pages, authorship and dates; distinguish complete, incomplete and empty nodes; trace external statements to primary sources; analyse the annotation and collaboration model; compare relation types and navigation; and record the site's philosophical commitments separately from its technical architecture.

Completion means a documented scope, item-level provenance, an account of what was excluded, and a comparator report explaining what The Necessary Tangle should learn and what it should avoid.

## 8. Canonical sources and archive locators

Register: [canonical-source-register.md](canonical-source-register.md)

The first register includes Ashby, Wiener, von Foerster/BCL, Beer, Bateson, ASC, system dynamics, the Santa Fe Institute's foundational-papers project and Principia Cybernetica.

The next work is not to add logos or landing pages. It is to connect particular statements to item-level records, pages, tapes, dates and archival identifiers, and to mark the role each source can legitimately play.

## 9. Bridge-building from the graph gaps

Grouping note: [emergent-categories.md](emergent-categories.md)

The sparse graph now supplies a direct research queue. Priority bridges include:

- method to concept;
- method to documented use;
- law or principle to primary text and practice;
- practitioner to declared influences;
- teacher to learner;
- institution and event to collaboration;
- concept to rival definition;
- source corpus to the statements actually derived from it.

Completion is not ‘everything connected’. It is a measurable reduction in isolated entries through typed, sourced relations that survive review.

## Ordering

The practical sequence is now:

1. stabilise the participation model, curator review and map interface;
2. complete a first itemised Foundational Papers inventory and comparator-map pass;
3. build the SysCoI/model.report ingestion and de-duplication rules;
4. deepen the Principia Cybernetica pass and canonical-source locators;
5. use the graph's isolated practice and law entries as the bridge-building queue;
6. develop several practitioner influence constellations with domain stewards;
7. run bounded Monoskop and company-knowledge discovery passes against explicit scope rules;
8. rerun clustering under several relation filters and publish how the picture changes.

New breadth should not outrun the ability to explain and source the connections already published.
