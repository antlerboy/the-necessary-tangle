# Hoverstadt and Velitchkov first pass

## Patrick Hoverstadt

Patrick Hoverstadt is represented as a systems practitioner, author and organisation-design consultant whose work develops applications of management cybernetics, the Viable System Model and systems approaches to strategy.

The first-pass constellation distinguishes:

- Patrick Hoverstadt, the person;
- *The Fractal Organization*;
- *Patterns of Strategy*;
- *The Grammar of Systems*;
- his public SCiO roles and teaching;
- Syntegrity and VSM-related practice where public sources support the connection.

The works are not collapsed into one generic ‘VSM’ association. Their arguments, publication histories and relationships to other people and traditions require separate source records.

## Ivo Velitchkov

Ivo Velitchkov is represented as a cybernetician and practitioner working on viability, requisite variety, autonomy and cohesion, organisational design, semantic knowledge representation and the public use of graph tools.

The first-pass constellation distinguishes:

- Ivo Velitchkov, the person;
- his public writing and SCiO contributions;
- the submitted viability/natural-drift correction in issue #21;
- Nodica as a separate software and semantic-graph project;
- documented conceptual and practice connections rather than inferred personal influence.

Neither person is included merely because the curator knows or values their work. Public entries need inspectable sources and typed statements.
