# Coverage programmes carried into iteration 0.12

The following programmes come directly from the current running feedback. They are part of the release backlog, not claims of exhaustive completion.

## Curator sources and reading list

Audit Benjamin P Taylor’s recommended reading list and public body of work against the graph. Record each item as represented, represented thinly, missing, deferred or out of scope. Public sources should support public statements; private or company material may guide discovery but is not itself silently published.

## SCiO practice corpus

Inventory SCiO’s systems methods, intervention skills, competency material, named authors, trainers and public resources. Distinguish a method from an intervention skill, a person from an institutional role, and SCiO’s own classifications from wider field consensus.

## Apprenticeship workbooks

Review the sources used in the apprenticeship workbooks in private company knowledge. Build a private candidate register, replace private discovery with legitimate public sources, and publish only material cleared for public use.

## Practitioner and community archives

Review Roger James’s public NotebookLM source collection, CYBCOM, Cybernetics Society and American Society for Cybernetics lists and archives. Mailing-list mention is evidence of circulation or discussion, not automatically of influence or validity.

## Arthur Battram

Develop Arthur Battram and *Navigating Complexity* as part of the UK practice lineage, with public bibliographic and contextual sources and links to the concepts and practices actually used.

## Completion discipline

Each programme needs an itemised inventory, provenance, admission and rejection decisions, identity resolution, and a visible completion test. Registering a corpus is not the same thing as covering it.
