# Curatorial model

The Necessary Tangle is a public atlas backed by a structured evidence graph. It is not a crowd-edited page system in which any visitor can overwrite the current account.

## The unit of review

The core curatorial unit is a precise statement:

- a concept definition;
- a historical or intellectual statement;
- a typed connection between two entries;
- a source record;
- an evidence locator;
- a challenge or competing account.

Each can be reviewed without treating the rest of an entry as indivisible.

## Public proposal, curatorial decision

Visitors propose corrections and challenges through GitHub Issues. Wider questions belong in Discussions. Accepted changes are made through versioned commits and releases.

The curator may reword a proposal so that it carries no more than the evidence supports. For example, ‘A influenced B’ may need to become ‘B cited A’, ‘A and B participated in the same event’, or ‘a later author compares A with B’.

## Curator does not mean sole author

Curatorship carries responsibility for selection, boundaries, wording, source quality, status, permissions and publication. It does not imply ownership of the knowledge represented, priority over the people cited, or the last word on a disputed field.

Contributors retain attribution through the public history where reasonably practicable. Domain stewards may recommend decisions in named areas. The curator remains accountable for what is merged and released.

## Categories are revisable observations

The atlas uses entity types, tags and temporary neighbourhood names so people can navigate it. It also analyses the evidence graph to see what groups arise from recorded relations.

Neither process is neutral. An emergent cluster is affected by:

- what the atlas has admitted or omitted;
- which sources have been researched;
- how relation types have been defined;
- which provisional or disputed relations are included;
- the clustering method and its parameters;
- the curator's interpretation when naming the cluster.

A cluster is therefore evidence about the graph and its curation, not immediate proof of a natural school of thought.

## Why this is not simply a wiki

GitHub supplies useful wiki-like functions: identity, discussion, revision history, issues and pull requests. The public product remains purpose-built because it needs typed graph relationships, paths, lineage layers, source-aware maps, guided conceptual journeys and visible uncertainty.

## Participation and authority

Participation roles are described in [membership and permissions](membership-and-permissions.md). Technical access is granted only where it is needed. LLMs and automations act under named human sponsorship and cannot merge or release autonomously.
