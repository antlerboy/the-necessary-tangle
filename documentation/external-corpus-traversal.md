# External corpus traversal

This record distinguishes traversal from evidential warrant. A landing page may establish that a project exists; a relationship is admitted only when an internal page, passage, abstract, paper or programme record supports a narrower claim. Page adjacency, index co-occurrence, meeting co-presence and title similarity are not treated as intellectual relationships.

The machine-readable companion is `external_corpus_review` in `data/public-data.json`.

## Principia Cybernetica

Traversed the introduction, cybernetic principles, self-organisation guide, metasystem-transition pages, project history, nutshell overview and navigation architecture. Followed the project history through CYBSYS-L discussions, conferences, adaptive semantic hypertext and project spin-offs.

Retained four bounded claims: the semantic network operationalises the project's account of self-organisation; a metasystem transition specialises emergence as a new higher level of control; and positive and negative feedback respectively amplify and stabilise variation in the project's self-organisation mechanism. The existing Natural drift tension remains important: Principia makes natural selection generative while Maturana and Mpodozis dispute that mechanism.

Uncertainty: these pages are primary for project self-description, not independent validation or a neutral history of cybernetics.

## David Ing and Coevolving Innovations

Traversed the publications and digest indexes, the Pattern Manual record and paper, Systems Changes Learning record and article, and the 1999 Ackoff-influence record and paper trail. Followed named references from the abstracts into the work-level records rather than inferring from the index.

Located the existing authorship and development statements more precisely. Added three work-level routes: the Pattern Manual operationalises multiple perspectives as named voices on issues; Systems Changes Learning specialises contextural action learning; and Ing's service-systems pattern form requires explicit voices.

The 1999 paper explicitly names three Ackoff works as shaping Ing's appreciation of enterprise design, but no edge was added because Ackoff is not currently an entity and adding a new person on one source would expand the bounded pass.

Uncertainty: first-party records establish authorship and intended concepts, not uptake or efficacy.

## Six comparison archives

### W. Ross Ashby Digital Archive

Traversed the journal index, 25-volume bookshelf, timeline, summaries, alphabetical and subject indexes, bibliography, 504-reference list, letters index, catalogue and biography sections. The archive exposes 7,189 journal pages, 1,600 index cards and more than 2,300 summaries, with page-image and British Library routes.

No semantic edge was added: index presence and correspondence establish documentary proximity, not influence or agreement. The archive also preserves Ashby's later warning that some early notes were inaccurate.

### American Society for Cybernetics

Traversed the Foundations history contents and its control, system, communication, knowledge, circularity and feedback sections, then the Archives Working Group, newsletter, Zotero and UIUC collection routes. Strengthened the existing Feedback to Cybernetics historical claim, but kept it provisional because the Wiener primary-page locator remains incomplete and the ASC history explicitly warns of multiple, contested histories.

### International Society for the Systems Sciences

Traversed the 1954 formation record, society name changes, annual themes, proceedings routes and journal routes. The record supports organisational chronology involving von Bertalanffy, Boulding, Rapoport and Gerard; it does not prove conceptual influence from co-presence. No semantic edge was added.

### International Federation for Systems Research

Traversed the Conversations legacy, programme principles, publication routes and federation overview. Added one explicitly interpreted, low-confidence example connecting systems convening with creating space for multiple perspectives. The source does not use the atlas's systems-convening label and dialogue does not demonstrate consensus.

### System Dynamics Society

Traversed the method guide, bibliography and literature-review routes, MIT collection route, and proceedings examples on stocks, flows, feedback and model boundaries. Replaced a legacy-unverified Feedback to System Dynamics edge with a precise definitional-prerequisite claim grounded in the Society's method description.

Uncertainty: an institutional account of method structure is not an efficacy evaluation and needs critical histories alongside it.

### Santa Fe Institute and Complexity Explorer

Traversed SFI's About and research pages, the Introduction to Complexity syllabus and FAQ, the resource index, and working-paper routes on emergence and evolution. Added one documentary curriculum edge for self-organisation and one interpreted, provisional framing of emergence as macro-level behaviour studied within complexity.

Uncertainty: SFI is a major institution, not a proxy for the entire field, and its vocabulary should not be imported wholesale into social practice.

## Pass decision

Eleven public source records were added and two existing records were repaired. Eight new edges and six existing edges were added or strengthened. Three corpus traversals deliberately produced no semantic edge. This is a better result than turning archive abundance into unsupported graph density.
