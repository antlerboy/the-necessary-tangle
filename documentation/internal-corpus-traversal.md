# Internal corpus traversal

This pass audits every maintained non-public source record without pretending that all records provide the same access or warrant. The source material is divided into four evidence classes. The machine-readable companion is `internal_corpus_review` in `data/public-data.json`.

## Inventory

Eighteen non-public source records are maintained:

- six supplied author decks with slide-level extracts and 17 direct crosswalk statements;
- five earlier author syntheses or lectures represented through prior extracts, evidence records or broad section locators;
- six legacy SCiO or professional-framework records used heavily as registers and discovery sources;
- one permission-limited network image retained for comparison and discovery only.

No claim gains strength merely because more than one of these records repeats it. Duplicate titles, generated crosswalk citation and visual proximity are not independent corroboration.

## Supplied slide-level material

### Systems convening and boundaries

The maintained extracts connect systems convening to the social conditions for systems change, then connect systems change to boundary choice and multiple perspectives. The three edges are now given claim-specific rationales. Scope remains narrow: an author teaching synthesis does not demonstrate intervention effectiveness.

### Five key questions for transformation

The one-slide framework connects transformation inquiry with purpose and systemic governance. It organises questions; it is not represented as a universal transformation sequence or causal law.

### Four dynamics for effective organisation

The cited slides connect the Organic Systems Framework with autonomy, cohesion and purpose through the dynamics of segmenting, blending, empowering and harmonising. The atlas retains those author-model connections without asserting that the four dynamics form a field-wide taxonomy.

### VSM simplification

The cited slides connect the teaching simplification of the Viable System Model with complexity, variety, adaptation and governance. The locators have been normalised and the rationale for each route is now explicit. A deliberate simplification is not presented as the full formal VSM.

### Clarity practices

The cited slides connect productive conversation with examining reasoning and action, and decision-structure design with preserving requisite decision variety. These are author practice claims; no independent outcome evaluation is implied.

### Better conversations

The cited slides connect the ladder of inference with observer selection and reinforcing feedback, and productive conversation with making different interpretations discussable. The feedback statement is a teaching synthesis, not an empirical causal estimate.

## Earlier author material

Core Thinking Integration, the systems-complexity-cybernetics operating manual, the outcomes-and-complexity presentation and the VSM lecture were reconciled with their existing edges and evidence records. Their strongest routes concern distinctions, boundary, observer, information, transduction, viability, autonomy, cohesion, variety handling and adaptation.

No new semantic edge was added from the outcomes presentation: the maintained data contains only a documentary source-navigation link. The duplicate Core Thinking Integration title is explicitly treated as one intellectual source, not two independent warrants.

The main remaining limitation is locator precision. The non-public originals cannot be inspected by readers, and several existing locators name only a lecture topic, boundary test or broad section.

## Legacy internal registers

The Jobson definitions note and Korycki presentation currently establish corpus membership only. The SCiO competency-framework material has a small number of section-level mappings. The SCiO CF Resources draft, Feedback SysBoK and Recursion SysBoK are cited by hundreds of generated crosswalk comparisons but provide very few claim-level locators.

This is recorded as provenance debt, not hidden as coverage. Generated crosswalk citations do not turn a broad internal guide into primary evidence for every comparison. Future work should replace high-value claims with public, claim-level sources.

## Discovery-only comparator

The Perko systems-researchers network image remains permission-limited and discovery-only. Its six uses were audited. Visual proximity in a network image does not establish influence, collaboration, agreement or intellectual lineage.

## Pass decision

All 18 internal records now have an explicit evidence class, decision and uncertainty. Seventeen supplied-deck relationships now have claim-specific rationales and normalised locators where encoding had degraded the ranges. No new relationship was added: the bounded improvement is clearer warrant, not denser graphing.
