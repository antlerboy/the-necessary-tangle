# Six SysPrac26 sessions: source decisions

Six attributed session accounts are admitted as candidates, subject to human editorial acceptance. The comparisons are editorial questions, not a common framework or evidence of influence. The recovered auto-generated captions were read, but neither audio nor on-screen visuals were independently checked against them. Timestamps identify caption passages. The resource descriptions were read; the S30 and S35 public decks and both S34 articles were also read. No member slide deck is reproduced. The public S38 and S33 listings say 'Contact the Author'; the S30, S34, S35, and S41 listings say 'Use with Accreditation'. Original attributed commentary and public links are used here; no open licence or author permission is asserted.

- S30, Kai-Uwe Radde, [Internalized Liabilities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfRfpuKoLw8), 09:49–10:33, 17:06–18:13, and 29:12–31:04; [public presentation](https://www.systemspractice.org/resources/attachment/0857f639-9f30-45b8-85fb-48eb63f05bf1), slides 14–20 and 29–36. Admit the six-debt management lens, subjective influence map, and reported team feedback questions. Defer taxonomy completeness, causal rankings, performance effects, and the AI conclusion; the management lens does not establish accounting recognition.
- S33, Max Shein, [Reducing Complexity or Relocating It?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C8k3DJcRt3k), 11:10–11:58, 13:07–15:19, and 18:32–22:28. Admit the situated hypotheses and the distinction between recognising a problem and sustaining action. Defer causal or current NHS claims; the member presentation was unavailable.
- S34, Ivo Velitchkov, [The Second Law of Technodynamics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wus7m9l6g6I), 12:31–17:19 and 20:45–24:21; [article](https://www.linkandth.ink/p/second-law-of-technodynamics), 'Coordination control', 'Counter-actions', and 'Endgames'; [Cohesion Spectrum v.2](https://www.linkandth.ink/p/cohesion-spectrum-v2), autonomy definitions and 'Nominal and Effective Autonomy'. Admit the attributed autonomy distinction and coordination-control proposition, deepening the existing Technodynamics record. The thermodynamics comparison is a mnemonic; the article does not establish concentration alone as a cause of collapse. The separately linked older [Productive Organisational Paradoxes](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/productive-organisational-paradoxes/129362759) deck is not this presentation. Defer unverified causal generalisation.
- S35, Jan De Visch, [Beyond Sustainability](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OzgfwbsFnxA), 11:55–15:24 and 21:53–22:54; [public presentation](https://www.systemspractice.org/resources/attachment/a955f354-d73d-4915-be53-7bbb35d00656), pages 2–7. Admit the attributed relationship-first and human-developmental governance account. Defer claims of demonstrated regenerative outcomes and independent claims about the organisations illustrated.
- S38, Steve Whitla, [Systems, purpose and the pursuit of shared meaning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2rVoAPcc9Rk), 09:38–12:40, 14:15–19:00, and 20:00–24:18. Admit the distinction between substantive and identity meaning, and the proposed questioning practices. The POSIWID example opens an inquiry, not a settled definition. Defer bibliographic attributions until primary references are checked; the member presentation was unavailable.
- S41, Mark Workman, [The application of Deep Uncertainty approaches](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pn4FMKBTe2Y), 05:33–06:50, 11:49–14:01, and 20:09–25:49. Admit the speaker's account of examining strategy failure conditions and challenging portfolio assumptions. Defer general claims about robust decision making and XLRM pending primary bibliography, as well as numerical and performance assertions: the member presentation and underlying models were unavailable.
