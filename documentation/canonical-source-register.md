# Canonical public source register

‘Canonical’ here means high-value for a stated task. It does not mean neutral, exhaustive or correct merely by inclusion.

Release `0.7-constellations-alpha` registers twelve public sources spanning publisher records, primary archives, institutional collections, bibliographies, proceedings and maintained project corpora.

## Registered sources

- *Foundational Papers in Complexity Science* official tables of contents and citation record;
- Principia Cybernetica’s official project site, introduction, systems-concepts network, dictionary, metasystem-transition account and Global Brain Group page;
- the W. Ross Ashby Digital Archive;
- the University of Illinois Cybernetics Thought Collective collection;
- the American Society for Cybernetics online library;
- the Cybernetics Society archive;
- the International Society for the Systems Sciences history and *World of Systems* material;
- International Federation for Systems Research Conversations;
- System Dynamics Society bibliography and proceedings;
- Santa Fe Institute research and press material;
- New England Complex Systems Institute resources.

Some of these appear as one register entry backed by several source records.

## Use and limit

Each register item states:

- its category and scope;
- what it is good evidence for;
- what it cannot establish on its own.

An official project page is strong evidence for a project’s stated programme, membership and terminology. It is not independent validation. A bibliography is strong for discovery and publication metadata, but does not prove influence. An archive can establish wording and chronology, but interpretation still requires context.

The register is a research infrastructure layer. It should widen across traditions, languages and regions rather than quietly hardening the current English-language institutional centre into a canon.
