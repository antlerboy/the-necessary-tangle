# Company-knowledge discovery: first private pass

A bounded first search has been run across relevant company knowledge. This was a discovery pass, not a publication pass.

No private URL, internal extract, client material or confidential document has been copied into the public repository or treated as public evidence.

## What the pass found

The private material is not one missing corpus waiting to be imported. It is a set of overlapping working collections developed for different purposes. The highest-yield groups were:

- systems laws, principles and disputed formulations;
- method selection, multimethod practice and quality checks;
- differentiation, integration, autonomy and cohesion;
- power, legitimacy, convening and participation;
- public-service outcomes, transformation and commissioning;
- teaching decks, working bibliographies and comparison notes.

This changes the diagnosis of the atlas. The main problem is not that the curator has no relevant material. It is that much of the material has not yet been converted into public, typed and inspectable statements with suitable sources and locators.

The private collections also contain useful disagreement. Candidate ‘laws’ vary from formal results and specialised scientific principles to heuristics, aphorisms and slogans. Methods are sometimes presented as rivals and sometimes as complementary. Claims about the unity or separateness of systems, cybernetics and complexity remain contested. Those differences must survive public-source replacement rather than being ironed into one synthetic doctrine.

## Public replacement queue

Before any discovery becomes atlas content:

1. Identify the smallest statement actually being proposed.
2. Find the original publication, public archive record or complete bibliographic citation.
3. Record a page, section, date, timestamp or archival locator.
4. Distinguish formal result, scientific principle, heuristic, practice rule, slogan and curatorial interpretation.
5. Connect each method to the concepts it uses, the work it is for, documented applications, limitations and serious counter-accounts.
6. Replace claims about the identity of the field with public evidence from the people and traditions making those claims.
7. Exclude client-confidential, personal, contractual and operational material.
8. Keep unresolved private discoveries outside the public dataset until the evidential replacement is adequate.

## Immediate public work arising from the pass

The next bridge-building work should concentrate on:

- laws and principles whose wording and source can be checked against originals;
- methods that already have public primary or authoritative sources but remain isolated in the graph;
- the relation between boundary, observer, purpose, power and intervention;
- the relation between requisite variety, autonomy, integration, recursion and viability;
- the distinction between complexity as a family of scientific programmes and systems practice as purposeful engagement;
- public-service practice accounts that show how concepts travel into action without treating use as proof of theoretical validity.

## Status

The first private discovery pass is complete. Public-source replacement, statement-level review and graph integration are still pending. Issue 8 remains the governing record for that work.
