# Iteration 0.12 release scope

This iteration responds to the latest curator comments and to site-generated contribution issue #21.

## People and works

- develop Ivo Velitchkov as a person entry and connect his public work on viability, requisite inefficiency, autonomy and cohesion;
- develop Patrick Hoverstadt as a person entry and represent *The Fractal Organization*, *Patterns of Strategy* and *The Grammar of Systems* as distinct works;
- distinguish the people, publications, concepts and documented relationships rather than adding a generic association.

## Submitted correction

Issue #21 is treated as a site-generated contribution by Ivo Velitchkov. Its proposal to distinguish viability from fitness, and natural drift from simplified ‘survival of the fittest’ language, is reviewed and incorporated with public sources and attribution.

## Semantics and comparison

The release documents the atlas’s explicit semantics: typed nodes, typed relation families, direction, evidential status, scope and provenance. Nodica is registered as a useful RDF and graph-visualisation comparator, not represented as the same kind of project.

## Intake

Release triage must reconcile three feeds:

1. the running curator-feedback thread;
2. site-generated contribution issues;
3. standing research issues.

A submission appearing in one feed must not disappear because another feed was used as the release notebook.

## Wider coverage programmes

SCiO methods, people and works; Benjamin P Taylor’s reading list and directly referenced public work; apprenticeship-workbook references; Roger James’s public NotebookLM source collection; CYBCOM, Cybernetics Society and ASC discussion archives; and Arthur Battram’s *Navigating Complexity* remain explicit source programmes. This iteration records and starts those programmes but does not claim exhaustive coverage where item-level review has not happened.

## Usability

The public site should offer a calm starting route before the full graph. The whole map remains available, but first-time readers should not have to confront the entire evidence graph without bearings.
