# Agent-assisted contributions

Automated tools can help with research organisation, comparison, transcription, code and drafting. They do not become members of The Necessary Tangle and do not acquire authority by producing fluent text.

## Named human responsibility

Every agent-assisted contribution must have a named human sponsor. The sponsor:

- chooses the task and its boundary;
- discloses the tool or model and the work it performed;
- checks the cited sources and any extracted data;
- reviews the exact diff before submission;
- removes private material, credentials and unsupported assertions;
- remains answerable for corrections after publication.

An agent must not push directly to the protected public account, approve its own work, merge its own pull request or represent itself as an independent project member.

## Evidence discipline

Agent-produced text is a proposal. It is not evidence. Public statements need the same source, locator, scope and status as human-drafted statements.

Do not infer influence, mentorship, chronology or priority from resemblance. Do not turn a project’s self-description into independent validation. Do not silently combine sources whose claims, dates or categories conflict.

## Public and private material

Never submit private email, internal company material, client information, credentials, private URLs or large copyrighted extracts. Private material may reveal a research lead, but the public contribution must replace it with a legitimate public source or a complete bibliographic citation marked ‘No public link’.

## Pull-request disclosure

State:

1. the tool or model used;
2. the task it performed;
3. the named human sponsor;
4. what was checked manually;
5. known uncertainty or remaining work.

The curator decides whether the contribution is accepted, revised, held, disputed or declined.
